## 1. Product today

- **International and English first.** Free for students worldwide; a paid workspace for schools, universities and training centers (pilot first, then 149 USD per month for up to 100 learners).
- **Problem.** Students find hackathons, grants and scholarships through chat links and random posts, and miss deadlines. Schools and training centers track their students in spreadsheets and chats.
- **Solution.** One radar of grants, hackathons and internships where every deadline is quoted from the organizer's own page; 235 skill quizzes with a Verified badge; a CV gap check against each listing; application prep checklists; deadline alerts in Telegram and Discord; a teammates network; and, for schools, a workspace with cohorts, invite links, a roster and a CSV export.
- **Distribution.** Students come first (free, no invite, profile on the device). Schools come second: the student base is the reason a school asks for a pilot.

## 2. Positioning for Y Combinator (target batch Spring or Summer 2027)

Framed on the owner's reading of YC Requests for Startups. Lead with one clear idea: a radar that only publishes opportunities whose deadline it can prove, plus a skill gap check that tells a student what to learn first. The three supporting theses:

- **"The 15 dollar an hour expert":** an ordinary school counselor with AI tools can work at the level of a private admissions agency for hundreds of students at 149 USD per month. Status: the schools workspace exists in an early version.
- **"Plaid for scholarships":** one verified student profile that schools and programs can trust. Status: undecided (50-50). It needs server accounts first and is not promised publicly.
- **"LLMs that write well":** CV and application help in natural language for a specific target. Status: the CV gap check is live; an essay helper is optional and later, started only by the student.

## 3. Scope decisions

- **Cut:** KakaoTalk, school ranking, employer cases, one-click apply (applications are submitted on the organizer's site), personal roadmap.
- **Undecided:** the verified portfolio (certificate and GitHub checks).
- **Optional, later, never up front:** an essay helper the student starts by pressing a button, with their topic and their own skills, labelled a draft.

## 4. Investor requirements

From the MOST Holding CEO and venture funds:

- Before revenue, the metric is month-over-month growth in CustDev interviews, pilots launched with schools and signed letters of intent (LOI), not only revenue.
- International from day one: the US, Europe, Japan and the wider APAC region, and global remote opportunities.
- A founding team with clear roles: product and full-stack, AI and backend for the radar core, and strong English for international networking.

## 5. Competition

`docs/COMPETITOR_HACKATHON_KZ.md` is a teardown of the closest local competitor: events and a portfolio with certificate checks, built around one country. Our openings: global scope, scholarships and grants, skill evidence and a gap check, and a schools workspace.
