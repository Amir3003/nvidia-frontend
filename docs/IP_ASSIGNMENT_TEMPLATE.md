# IP assignment agreement: template

Draft for a lawyer to adapt to Kazakhstan law (or the law of the company's country). Not legal advice. Each founder and contributor signs a separate copy once the company is registered. Fill every `[__]`.

---

**Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement**

Date: [__]

Between:

1. **[Company legal name]**, registered in [__] under number [__], address [__] (the "Company"); and
2. **[Full name]**, ID or passport [__], address [__] (the "Assignor").

**1. Background.** Before and after the Company was registered, the Assignor took part in creating the product known as QADAM Hub, including its source code, database schema, bots, radar pipeline, quiz content, designs, logo, texts, marketing materials and related documentation (the "Work"). The Work is held in the repository [repository URL] and related accounts.

**2. Assignment.** The Assignor assigns to the Company, worldwide and for the full term of protection, all intellectual property rights the Assignor has in the Work, created up to the date of this Agreement, including copyright, the exclusive right to use, modify, distribute and license the Work, and any rights in trademarks, domain names and accounts created for the Work.

**3. Future work.** Work the Assignor creates for the Company after this date belongs to the Company under the Assignor's employment or service contract, which shall contain a matching clause.

**4. Moral rights.** The Assignor keeps the right to be named as an author where the law makes that right non-transferable, and agrees not to object to modifications of the Work by the Company.

**5. Third-party materials.** The Assignor confirms that, to the best of the Assignor's knowledge, the Work contains no third-party material except open source components and services listed in `docs/IP_REGISTER.md`, and that parts produced with AI assistants were produced under terms that give the output to the user.

**6. Consideration.** [Shares / payment / other consideration: __].

**7. Further steps.** The Assignor will sign any document reasonably needed to record or enforce this assignment, and will hand over access to repositories, domains and accounts used for the Work.

**8. Confidentiality.** The Assignor keeps non-public parts of the Work and Company information confidential.

**9. Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of [__]. Disputes go to [__].

Company: ______________________ (name, title, signature)

Assignor: ______________________ (name, signature)
